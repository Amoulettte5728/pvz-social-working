Normal Adventure mission flag cleanup

For normal Adventure levels (the enabled missions after Tutorial 1-3):
- type is forced to 1
- modeId is forced to 1
- isTutorial is explicitly false
- no tutorial mode 3/4/5 is allowed to leak into I1007 or I1008
- existing CardSelectorHelper/card-selection fields remain enabled

Tutorial 1-3 retain their original tutorial types/modes so their scripted tutorial flow is unchanged.
Account-level tutorialStep/skippedTutorial state is intentionally retained because it controls progression and town/tutorial completion; it is not used as the per-level mission mode.
