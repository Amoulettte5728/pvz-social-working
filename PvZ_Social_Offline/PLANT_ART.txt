TOWN PLANT ART
==============

Plant_Peashooter.swf, Plant_Repeater.swf, Plant_SnowPeashooter.swf and
Plant_Sunflower.swf are the town plant graphics made by a friend of the
player. Each one exports a MovieClip class with the same name as the file.
The frames follow the growth stages in conf_1_.xml <plantAnimator>:

  Peashooter / Repeater / Snow Pea (80 frames): 1-20 seedling, 21-50 growing, 51-80 grown
  Sunflower (70 frames):                         1-20 seedling, 21-45 growing, 46-70 grown

item_105_1_.png and item_118_1_.png (Snow Pea and Repeater shop icons) were
cut from the grown frames of these files.

To add another plant later: put Plant_<Name>.swf next to main.swf, add it to
config.gz (or re-run build_config.py, which picks up every .swf in the folder),
and make sure its seed id is in _SEED_DEFS and _SEEDS_WITH_ART in server.py.
