PLAY ON ANDROID / OTHER DEVICES
===============================

One server on your PC; any number of phones, tablets and browsers can play
at the same time, each on its own account.

First time only
  1. Run Allow_Phone_Access.bat and click Yes (opens port 9090 in the
     Windows firewall for Private networks).
  2. Make sure Windows treats your home Wi-Fi as a Private network.

Every time
  1. Start the server on the PC (Start_Server.bat or the launcher).
     The launcher's bottom line and the server log show the address for
     phones, e.g.  http://192.168.1.23:9090/
  2. On the phone (same Wi-Fi), open that address in Chrome or Firefox.
  3. Log in or create an account, then press "Play in browser".
  4. Tap the play button once it appears (this also turns sound on).
     Press "Full screen" and turn the phone sideways.

Notes
  - Each browser/device stays logged in to its own account. Logging in on a
    phone does NOT change which account the PC launcher plays.
  - The game runs through Ruffle, a Flash emulator bundled in ruffle/, and a
    Chinese font bundled in fonts/. No internet connection is needed.
  - First load on a phone downloads ~15 MB (emulator); after that it is cached.
  - Don't play the same account on two devices at once - the last one to
    save wins.
  - To play away from home, use a private VPN such as Tailscale on the PC and
    the phones, and open http://<PC's Tailscale address>:9090/. Do NOT forward
    port 9090 on your router: the server has no real security.
