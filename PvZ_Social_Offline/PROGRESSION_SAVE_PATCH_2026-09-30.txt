PvZ Social Offline — persistent levels + plant unlocks
Build: 2026-09-30-official-projectiles-persistent-progress

BASE
----
This update is based directly on the user's PvZ_Social_Offline_official_projectiles.zip.
The projectile SWF and the three core SWFs were not rebuilt for this progression patch.

SAVE DATA
---------
Each local or account save now has:
  levelsFinished  : missions that have actually been won
  unlockedLevels  : missions currently available (mission 1 + the next mission after every win)
  unlockedPlants  : plant card resource IDs currently owned/unlocked

Starting plant cards:
  12 = Peashooter
   2 = Sunflower

PLANT REWARDS
-------------
Completing a mission adds the following plant card:
  Mission 1 -> 17 Cherry Bomb
  Mission 2 ->  9 Wall-nut
  Mission 3 -> 18 Potato Mine
  Mission 4 -> 16 Snow Pea
  Mission 5 -> 20 Chomper
  Mission 6 -> 24 Repeater
  Mission 7 -> 13 Puff-shroom
  Mission 8 ->  1 Sun-shroom
  Mission 9 -> 14 Fume-shroom
  Mission 10 -> 5 Grave Buster

MISSION PANEL
-------------
services.I1007 now supplies missions 1..10. Each mission after #1 has the
previous mission in preMissions, so the Adventure panel can reveal the next
mission after its prerequisite is completed.

services.I1001 returns only unlocked plant cards in items["0"]. All plant
catalog entries remain available to the Almanac, but only saved unlocked
cards are treated as owned by the client.

PERSISTENCE
-----------
A normal TD win (services.I4003) saves the completed mission, unlocks the next
mission, and grants its plant reward.
The tutorial/build-complete callback (services.I1016) is treated as mission 1
completion and uses the same persistence path. I1016Skip/I1016Accept also mark
mission 1 complete so an account that skips/accepts the tutorial is not stuck.

MIGRATION
---------
Older saves without the new fields are migrated in memory. Existing
levelsFinished entries reconstruct their corresponding plant rewards, while
Peashooter and Sunflower are always retained as the starting cards.

STATIC DATA
-----------
Both swf/20110510/conf/MissionList.xml and swf/20130627/conf/MissionList.xml
now contain missions 1..10 with sequential preMission links.
