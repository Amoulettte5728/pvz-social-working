SEED PACKET + PVZ UI + BOOST BUILDING LINK FIX

This build materializes the generated config.gz resources under local/swf/v1/, which is the URL namespace the offline config actually references. In particular this makes pvzUI_1_.swf, ui_1_.swf, PVZEntry_1_.swf, pvzTD_1_.swf, and the five funchouse boost-building SWFs addressable at their configured URLs.

Verified pvzUI_1_.swf exports the card-selection symbols SmallCardContainer, BigCardContainer, CardContainer, MC_CardEffect and cardmask.

Verified funchouse SWFs export their configured animation symbols:
- funchouse1_1_Level1_Card / Level2_Card
- funchouse2_1_Level1_Card / Level2_Card
- funchouse3_1_Level1_Card / Level2_Card
- funchouse4_1_Level1_Card / Level2_Card
- funchouse5_1_Level1_Card / Level2_Card

Plant card ids and town seed packet ids are explicitly linked in server.py. Examples: Peashooter 12 -> seed 102, Sunflower 2 -> 101, Cherry Bomb 17 -> 106, Snow Pea 16 -> 105, Repeater 24 -> 118.

The normal Adventure I1008 response now exposes cardList entries with id/itemId/plantId/seedId/seedResourceId plus seedPackets. Tutorial modes remain unchanged.

Note: the project does not contain the original BoostData.xml, so this patch does not invent its internal XML schema. The actual boost-building SWFs and their configured symbols are made loadable.
