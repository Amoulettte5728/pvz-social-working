Seed selection + shop crash fix (build 2026-10-02-seed-select-shop-fix)
======================================================================
Both fixes are in server.py only. No SWF changed.

1. Normal adventure levels (前院1-1 ...) stopped after the opening camera pan.
   The seed bar (SCardListTopPanel.refreshSlot) reads, for each of the 10
   slots, pvzData["unlockRules"]["3"][slot]["levelRequired"].
   (3 = DataManager.UNLOCK_TYPE_CARD_SLOT; a seed-slot ITEM is type 10, its
   UNLOCK rules are type 3.) The login data had no such table, so the screen
   failed with "#1010 accessing field 0" and the level never reached the
   seed selection.
   -> _seed_slot_unlock_rules(): slots 1-6 need no level, slots 7-10 open at
      player levels 10/20/30/40 (offline choice; original values unknown).
   Result: 前院1-1 shows "请选择你的植物" with your owned plants, the 6-slot
   seed bar, 立刻开战!, the boost panel; then the battle starts with the full
   lawn, 5 mowers, seed bar + shovel, 菜单/speed buttons and the progress bar.

2. Item shop crash (#1009 in ItemShopListItem.isFullOwnItemLimit).
   The login data owns seed-slot items 9000-9009 (items["10"]), but the item
   catalogue (itemSettings) had no entries for them; the shop looks every
   owned item up and reads .groupId without a null check.
   -> itemSettings["10"] now has inactive, group-0 entries for 9000-9009
      (never sold, never counted as buildings).

Tested (Ruffle, copy of this folder): shop opens, the 住宅 tab lists the houses;
house -> 前院1-1 -> 确定 -> camera pan -> seed selection -> 3 seeds chosen ->
立刻开战! -> battle running (I4002 logged for mission 110, easy).
Not yet tested: playing 前院1-1 to the win / reward screen.
