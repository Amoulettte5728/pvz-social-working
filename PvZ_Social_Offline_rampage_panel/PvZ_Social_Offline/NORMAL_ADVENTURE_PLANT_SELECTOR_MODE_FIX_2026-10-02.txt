Normal Adventure plant selector mode fix — 2026-10-02

The client PVZEntry_1_.swf contains the real selector flow. In AdventureGame.finishSceneMoveToRight(), it calls CardSelectorHelper.instance.moveShow() unless Tutorial.isTutorial1, isTutorial2, or isTutorial3 is true. Those getters compare MissionVO.modeId against GAME_MODE_TUTORIAL1/2/3.

The offline server previously advertised type=1 for normal missions but did not explicitly provide modeId. This build adds modeId=1 for all normal Adventure missions and preserves 3/4/5 for the tutorial missions. It also supplies MissionVO-style aliases sceneId, allowedZombiesArr, appearZombieIdArr, initPlant, forcedCards, and forbiddenCards.

I1007 mission rows now also include type/modeId/isTutorial so the client carries the correct mode when building its mission data.

Tutorial missions 1-3 remain tutorial modes and do not show the selector. Normal Adventure mission 4 onward is ordinary mode and should reach the existing pvz_ui/CardSelectorHelper plant-selection screen.
