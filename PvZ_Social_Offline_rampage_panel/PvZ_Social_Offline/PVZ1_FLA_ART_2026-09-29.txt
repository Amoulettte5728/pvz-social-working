UPDATE: plants and mowers re-centred (build 2026-09-29-pvz1-fla-art-centered)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem: the FLA art uses PvZ 1's own origin, but the battle places each plant
clip at (cell corner + PLANT_OFFSET_XY_LIST offset), and those offsets were made
for the old Social Edition art (they range from [-32,-35] to [44,46]). So plants
sat off-centre and low, and the mower hung below its row.

Fix (in pvzTD_1_.swf only, no code changes):
- Every plant is measured on the first frame of its idle animation, counting only
  its visible pixels. It is then shifted so it is centred horizontally in its
  75 px cell, with its base 76 px below the cell top (cell height 95). Coffee
  Bean's base is at 60, so it sits on a mushroom.
- Pumpkin and Clam Chuck back halves move with their fronts.
- The Lawn Mower and Pool Cleaner are drawn at 90% size, sitting in their row just
  inside the left screen edge.
- All shifts are listed in td_art_generator (make_td.py writes <out>.shifts.json).
  To nudge one plant, change CELL_CX / BASE_Y / BASE_Y_SPECIAL / MOWER_CENTER
  in make_td.py and run it again.
Tested (Ruffle): level 1, the Peashooter stands centred in its tile and the mower is
in the row at the lawn's left edge.

UPDATE 2026-09-30: why the mower still looked low (build 2026-09-30-centered-launcher-fix)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The screenshot matched the OLD pvzTD_1_.swf exactly (mower top about 28 px
above the grass edge, Peashooter about 17 px right of the tile centre). The
cause: launcher.py reused ANY server already running on port 9090 ("Running
(existing)"). A server left running (hidden, pythonw) from the previous folder
kept serving that folder's files.

Fix:
- server.py answers GET /__build with {"label", "folder"}.
- launcher.py asks the running server which folder it serves. If it is another
  folder (or an old build that doesn't answer), it stops that server and
  starts this folder's own server.
The art itself is unchanged from "-centered".

UPDATE 2026-09-30: placement round 2 (build 2026-09-30-placement-2)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Compared against a PvZ 1 screenshot:
- Plants are now centred on their BASE: the bottom quarter of the visible
  plant (leaves and stem). Shooters' heads stick out forward like in PvZ, and
  round plants stay centred.
- Plant centre point is 30.5 px into the 75 px grid cell (the lawn's tile stripes
  start 10 px left of the code grid and are about 77 px wide).
- Plants raised 9 px: base 67 px below the cell top (Coffee Bean 51).
- Lawn Mower / Pool Cleaner: 80% size, raised about 10 px, moved left onto the
  pavement strip. A sliver is cut off at the screen edge, like the original
  Social mower.
Settings are in td_art_generator/make_td.py: CELL_CX, BASE_Y, BASE_Y_SPECIAL,
MOWER_CENTER, MOWER_SCALE.
