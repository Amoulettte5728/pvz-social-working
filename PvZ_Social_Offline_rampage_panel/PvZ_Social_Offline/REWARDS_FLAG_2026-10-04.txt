2. Flag zombie flag (pvzTD_1_.swf Prop_Flag1-3):
   The game draws the flag zombie's body (pvzNormalZombie Zombie_Flag) with the
   arm stretched forward and the fist at shoulder height - not like Zombie.fla,
   whose hand the flag used to follow, so the flag hung in the wrong place.
   The fist position was measured on every frame of the game's own body clip
   (td_art_generator/flag_fist.json). The pole is now held near its bottom end
   in that fist, long enough to reach above the head; the cloth is smaller
   (60%) and flies at the top; your fist image is drawn over the grip.
   Settings: FLAG_GRIP / FLAG_TOP / FLAG_SCALE / FLAG_CLOTH_SCALE in
   td_art_generator/make_td.py.
   pvzTD_1_.swf is updated in BOTH places (root and local/swf/v1/ - the game
   loads the local/swf/v1 copy).

Note: td_art_generator/make_td.py again contained flag settings that were
not mine (FLAG_OFFSET / FLAG_DRAW_HAND); they are replaced by the above.
