Poses and how the game uses them:
  - Wall-nut, Tall-nut, Vine Nut, Pumpkin and Clam Chuck show their three damage states.
  - Shooters recoil when firing. Mushrooms darken and sway slowly while asleep.
  - Potato Mine: buried, then armed with a blinking light.
  - Chomper and Super Chomper: idle, open, bite, chew.
  - Pults swing the basket. Torchwood and Flamewood cycle their flames.
  - Coffee Bean uses its own 12-frame animation.
  - Tangle Kelp uses its grab frames. Cherry Bomb, Jalapeno and Pickled Pepper swell before exploding.

Not from sprites (still simple placeholders): Marigold (no sprites supplied),
all bullets, lawn mower, sun, effects. Send the mower and sun zips and they
go in the same way.

Ice-shroom (id 38): the battle code has no Ice-shroom class, so it cannot be
planted in levels even with art.

Rebuild: td_art_generator/  ->  python make_td.py  (needs Python 3 + Pillow)
  poses.py      one function per plant: which part, where, scale, rotation
  make_td.py    which pose is shown on which game frame
  plant_sprites/ your sprite folders (unchanged)
The generator reproduces pvzTD_1_.swf byte-for-byte.
