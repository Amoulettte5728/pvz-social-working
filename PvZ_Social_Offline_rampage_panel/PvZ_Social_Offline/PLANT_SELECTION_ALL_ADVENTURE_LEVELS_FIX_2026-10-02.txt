Plant-selection / normal Adventure level fix — 2026-10-02

Goal:
- Show the normal card/plant selection flow for every non-tutorial Adventure mission.
- Keep Tutorials 1-3 on their existing tutorial flow.
- Stop the town PlantBuilding path from receiving a seed whose Plant_*.swf art is absent.

Changes:
1. _town_plants() now filters planted seed records against _SEEDS_WITH_ART before
   returning them to services.I2001. This prevents PlantBuilding from attempting
   to load missing Plant_*.swf symbols and reaching BitmapUtil.drawMovieClip(null),
   which is the direct failure shown by the reported Error #1009 stack.
2. Completed/skipped tutorial migration now also owns Cherry Bomb (plant/card 17),
   so the first normal Adventure level has a real owned plant for its selector.
3. I1008 now explicitly reports isTutorial/showCardSelector/cardSelectionEnabled,
   plus ownedPlants and cardSlots, for non-tutorial missions. Existing client logic
   still uses the mission type (3/4/5 tutorial; 1 normal Adventure) as the primary
   compatibility field.

Tutorial behavior is unchanged: Tutorials 1-3 do not request the normal card selector.
Normal Adventure missions (mission 4 through the last enabled mission) are marked for
card selection.

- ZIP integrity checked with unzip -t.
- All Plant_*.swf files referenced by _SEEDS_WITH_ART are present in the package.
- The previous Cherry Bomb animation/root-link repair remains in the package.
