Cherry Bomb / Wall-nut / level unlocking (build 2026-10-02-cherry-wallnut-level-chain)
====================================================================================
No SWF changed: conf/PlantData.xml (+ lang/zh-TW copy) and server.py only.

1. Plant stats were placeholders for EVERY plant: att 20, hp 300, recharge 7.5 s.
   The Cherry Bomb's 3x3 range was already right (rows above/same/below, one
   square left/right), but it hit for 20, so a 270 HP zombie survived.
   Original PvZ values now:
     damage 1800 : Cherry Bomb, Jalapeno, Potato Mine, Squash, Tangle Kelp,
                   Pickled Pepper
     health 4000 : Wall-nut, Pumpkin, Vine Nut, Clam Chuck
     health 8000 : Tall-nut
     recharge 30 s: Wall-nut, Potato Mine, Squash, Tangle Kelp, Pumpkin,
                    Tall-nut, Sea-shroom, Vine Nut, Clam Chuck
     recharge 50 s: Cherry Bomb, Jalapeno, Ice-shroom, Gatling Pea,
                    Gloom-shroom, Cattail, Winter Melon, Twin Sunflower,
                    Pickled Pepper
     everything else stays 7.5 s.
   The client reads these directly (PVZXmlParser: att -> plantAtt,
   hp -> maxHP, refreshTime -> card recharge).
   Tested: a Cherry Bomb planted diagonally in front of a zombie burns it
   to ash. Slow plants start the level recharging, as in PvZ.
   Wall-nut art: already the PvZ 1 FLA (Wallnut.fla), with its cracked stages.

2. New levels did not unlock after winning 前院1-1.
   progressId (the level's place in the catalogue) was counted per lawn type,
   so 前院1-1 had progressId 1 (= tutorial 1). Winning it saved "tutorial 1
   finished" and 前院1-2 never unlocked. Now:
     新兵训练1-3 = 1-3, 前院1-1..1-6 = 4-9, 水池1-1..1-6 = 10-15.
   The client checks completion with the real mission ids (110 = 前院1-1
   easy, 111 normal, 112 hard ...). The login unlock table now lists those
   too (missionsFinished in the save, plus the easy id of every finished
   adventure level from older saves). Wins pay the coins/exp of the
   difficulty actually played.
   Tested: after 前院1-1, the panel shows its trophy and 前院1-2 unlocked.

Night levels (前院2-x, 水池2-x) are in conf/missions.json but stay off: the
night lawn files pvzScreen2 / pvzScreen4 are not in the folder.
